In short

  • Your notes, minutes, transcripts and to-dos are stored only on your phone (and in your own backup, see below).
  • All AI features of OG Summary are built with Mistral AI, an AI provider based in France (EU).
  • All necessary data is processed on our server in Frankfurt am Main.
  • Recordings are only used for transcription and then deleted from the server and from your phone.
  • Your content is not used to train AI models.
  • We do not sell data and show no ads.

Data we process

Account

  • Email address (when signing in with email) or an anonymous guest account
  • The name you enter in the app
  • When you confirmed the recording notice, and the app version
  • Your plan (Free, Pro, Max) and its term
  • Usage counters: analyses and recording minutes per week, minutes from packs

Recordings and analysis

  • All AI features (transcription, speaker detection, summary and to-dos) run exclusively on Mistral AI. No other AI providers are used.
  • The audio file is sent encrypted to our server in Frankfurt am Main and passed on from there to Mistral AI for transcription.
  • Right after transcription the audio is deleted from the server and from your phone.
  • For the summary and to-dos, the transcript is sent to Mistral AI via our server. The result is returned to your phone and not stored on the server.
  • Mistral AI processes content only on our behalf, within the EU, and does not use it for training.

Purchases

  • Subscriptions and packs are handled by Google Play. We do not receive payment details.
  • We store the purchase token, product, order number and term to activate your plan.

Backing up your content

  • Android automatically backs up your content and settings to your own Google account (Android backup) if backup is enabled on your phone. We have no access to this backup. Audio is never backed up.
  • You can also export a backup file. You decide where to store it.

Contact requests

  • If you write to us via the contact form, we store your email address, message, app version and plan in order to reply.

Where data is stored

  • Server: Supabase, data center in Frankfurt am Main (EU). All necessary data is processed here.
  • AI (transcription and analysis): Mistral AI, France (EU)
  • Purchases: Google Ireland Limited (Google Play)
  • Android backup: in your own Google account

Legal basis

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): account, analysing recordings, plan and purchases
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): abuse prevention, answering requests

Retention

  • Audio: until transcription, then deleted from the server and your phone
  • Account data and counters: as long as your account exists
  • Purchase data: according to statutory retention periods
  • Contact requests: until resolved, at most 12 months

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection. Contact us via the contact form. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

Deleting your account

Write to us via the contact form and we will delete your account and all related data on the server. To remove the data on your phone, uninstall the app.
