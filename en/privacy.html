2. Summary

  • Your meetings, transcripts, minutes, notes and to-dos are stored on your phone, not on our server.
  • Recordings (audio) are only used for transcription and deleted afterwards.
  • With on-device transcription (always on Free, optional on Pro and Max) the audio never leaves your phone.
  • With cloud transcription (Pro and Max) the audio is sent via our server in Frankfurt am Main to Mistral AI (France, EU).
  • For summaries and to-dos, only the text of the transcript is sent to Mistral AI.
  • Your content is not used to train AI models, neither by us nor by Mistral AI.
  • We use no advertising, no tracking and no analytics tools and do not sell data.

3. Legal bases

  • Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR – performance of the contract for using the app.
  • Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR – compliance with legal obligations, e.g. tax and commercial retention duties.
  • Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR – legitimate interests, e.g. security and stability of our services, abuse prevention and answering enquiries.
  • § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG – access to information on your device where strictly necessary for the app function you requested (e.g. storing your content and settings on the device).

4. Processing in detail

4.1 Downloading the app

The app is provided via Google Play. When you download it, data such as your Google account, time of download and device identifiers are processed by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, under its own responsibility (policies.google.com/privacy).

4.2 User account and sign-in

Cloud transcription, summaries and plans require a user account. You can create an account with email and password or start as a guest without providing any details.

  • Data: email address (if provided), password (stored only as an encrypted hash), a random user ID, the name you enter in the app, time of registration and last sign-in, technical sign-in logs (time, IP address).
  • Purpose: providing the account, assigning plan and quota, protecting against unauthorised access and abuse.
  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; for sign-in logs Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in the security of the service).
  • Retention: as long as your account exists. Sign-in logs are deleted after 7 days at the latest.

We use your name so the AI can tell which tasks from a meeting concern you. For this it is sent to Mistral AI together with the transcript (see 4.5).

4.3 Confirmation of the recording notice

On first start you confirm that you will inform all participants before every recording. We store the time of this confirmation and the app version to be able to prove that the notice was given (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Retention: as long as your account exists.

4.4 Recording and transcription

The app only uses your phone's microphone once you start a recording and have granted permission. During recording, the audio is stored in the app's protected private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot access.

a) On-device transcription (always on Free, optional on Pro and Max)

The audio is converted to text directly on your phone using an open-source speech model. It is not transferred and is deleted from the phone after transcription. Speakers are not distinguished.

b) Cloud transcription (Pro and Max, default)

  • Data: audio file of the recording, recording length, user ID, recording ID.
  • Process: the audio is transferred encrypted (TLS) to our server at Supabase in Frankfurt am Main, stored there briefly and sent to Mistral AI SAS, 15 rue des Halles, 75001 Paris, France, for transcription and speaker detection. The result (text with timestamps and speaker labels) is returned to your phone.
  • Deletion: immediately after successful transcription the audio is deleted from our server and from your phone. If a transcription is aborted, an automatic process deletes remaining audio files on the server after 24 hours at the latest. The transcript is not stored on our server.
  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

4.5 Summary and to-dos

For minutes, title and to-dos, the text of the transcript together with the name you entered in the app is sent via our server to Mistral AI. The result is returned to your phone and not stored on our server. Contents of transcripts and results are not recorded in our server logs. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

4.6 About Mistral AI

Mistral AI processes the transmitted content as a processor under a contract pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR, solely to provide transcription and analysis. Under Mistral AI's terms, content processed via its API is not used to train AI models. Mistral AI may retain inputs and outputs for up to 30 days under its terms to detect abuse and deletes them afterwards. Processing takes place in the EU.

4.7 Personal data of other participants

Recordings usually contain voices and statements of other people. You as the user are responsible for the lawfulness of the recording: you must inform all participants before recording and obtain their consent.

  • If you use the app purely privately, the GDPR generally does not apply to your own processing (Art. 2(2)(c) GDPR).
  • If you use the app professionally, you are the controller of the participants' data. For cloud processing we then act on your behalf. A data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR) is available on request.

4.8 No automated decision-making

The AI analysis creates suggestions (minutes, to-dos) that you can freely edit. No automated decision with legal effect within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR takes place.

4.9 Usage quota and plan

  • Data: plan (Free, Pro, Max), term, number of summaries and recorded minutes per week, credit from minute packs, IDs of analysed recordings with date and length.
  • Purpose: accounting for the quota, activating the plan, preventing abuse.
  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR.
  • Retention: as long as your account exists. No recording content is included.

4.10 Purchases (subscriptions and minute packs)

Subscriptions and packs are purchased and paid via Google Play. We do not receive payment details. To activate your plan we store the purchase token, product ID, order number, purchase time and term, and verify the purchase via Google's interface (Google Play Developer API).

  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; for retention Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR.
  • Retention: as long as your account exists. Settlement reports we receive from Google about sales are retained for the statutory periods (usually 10 years, § 147 AO, § 257 HGB).

4.11 Speech model for on-device transcription

For on-device transcription the app downloads a speech model once from GitHub (GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). As with any download, your IP address is transmitted to GitHub. None of your content is transferred. GitHub participates in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; the transfer is based on the European Commission's adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

4.12 Notifications and reminders

Notifications (e.g. ongoing recording, to-do reminders) are generated locally on your phone only. We do not use push services and do not transfer any data for this.

4.13 Backing up your content

  • Android backup: if enabled in your Android settings, Android backs up your content and settings to your own Google account, under Google's responsibility. We have no access to this backup. Audio and speech models are excluded.
  • Backup file: you can export your content as a file. You decide where to store it. The file is not encrypted.

4.14 Contact form

If you write to us via the contact form, we process your email address, your message, your user ID, the app version and your plan to answer your request.

  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where the request concerns the contract, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in answering).
  • Retention: until the request is resolved, at most 12 months, unless retention duties apply.

If you email us directly, we process the data of your email in the same way. Our mailbox is operated by Google (Gmail).

4.15 Server logs

When our server functions are called, technically necessary access data is logged (time, IP address, function called, status code, duration). Contents of recordings, transcripts and results are not logged. The purpose is to ensure operation and security (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Logs are deleted after 7 days at the latest.

4.16 Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app under Settings → Account → Account status → Delete account. Your account and all related data on our server are deleted, except data we must retain by law (e.g. Google settlement reports on sales). Optionally all content on your phone is removed as well. Without the app installed, you can request deletion by email; see the Delete account page.

4.17 This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes technically necessary data, in particular your IP address, to deliver the page and ensure security (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub participates in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. The website uses no cookies, no analytics tools and no externally loaded fonts.

6. Transfers to third countries

Your data is generally processed in the EU. Our hosting provider Supabase is based in Singapore with parts of its team in the USA; data is stored in Frankfurt am Main, but access from these countries (e.g. for support or maintenance) cannot be ruled out. EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR) are in place as appropriate safeguards. For GitHub, the adequacy decision for the EU-US Data Privacy Framework applies (Art. 45 GDPR). You can request a copy of the safeguards from us.

7. Retention overview

  • Audio: until transcription; deleted from the server after 24 hours at the latest
  • Transcripts, minutes, notes, to-dos: only on your phone, until you delete them or uninstall the app
  • Account data, plan, quota: as long as your account exists
  • Sign-in and server logs: at most 7 days
  • Purchase data: as long as your account exists; Google settlement reports for the statutory periods (usually 10 years)
  • Contact requests: at most 12 months

8. Data security

We protect your data with technical and organisational measures (Art. 32 GDPR), in particular:

  • encrypted transfer (TLS) between app, server and Mistral AI
  • access restrictions at database level, so every user can only access their own data
  • secret keys only on the server, never in the app
  • data minimisation: content is only processed as long as needed for the function

9. Obligation to provide data

Providing your data is neither legally nor contractually required. Without a user account and the transfer of the recording or transcript, however, cloud features, summaries and plans cannot be used.

10. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21 GDPR).

Right to object: where we process data based on legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), you may object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation.

A message via the contact form or by email is sufficient to exercise your rights.

Right to lodge a complaint: you can lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The authority responsible for us is the Unabhängiges Datenschutzzentrum Saarland, Fritz-Dobisch-Straße 12, 66111 Saarbrücken, Germany.

11. Minimum age

The app is intended for persons aged 16 and over. Persons under 16 may only use the app with the consent of their parents or guardians.

12. Changes to this privacy policy

We update this privacy policy when the app, the services used or the legal situation change. The current version is available in the app and on this website. We inform you about material changes in the app.
