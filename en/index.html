Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to tell participants?

Yes. Please tell everyone before recording. It is required by law and it is fair.

Which languages are supported?

German and English work especially well, many other languages work too.

How long does the summary take?

Usually one to three minutes after stopping. You can close the app meanwhile.

What happens to my content on a new phone?

Android backs it up to your Google account. You can also export a backup file any time.

How do I cancel a subscription?

In the Play Store under Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
