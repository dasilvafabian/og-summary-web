Delete account

This page applies to the app OG Summary.

In the app

  • Open OG Summary and tap the gear icon at the top of the Record page.
  • Under Account, choose Delete account and confirm.
  • Optionally, also delete all content on your phone.

Without the app

If the app is no longer installed, email us at dasilvafabian31@gmail.com with the subject "Delete account" and the email address of your account. We delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address or guest account) and your name
  • Plan, usage counters and pack minutes
  • Stored purchase records (purchase token, product, term)
  • Audio files on the server that have not been processed yet

Notes, minutes and to-dos are stored only on your phone and in your own Android backup. They are removed when you uninstall the app or choose "Also delete all content on this device".

What is kept

  • Contact requests you sent us, without a link to your account, at most 12 months
  • Billing records at Google Play according to statutory retention periods

Active subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Please cancel it first in the Play Store under Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
